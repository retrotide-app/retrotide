# Acceptable use policy

_Last updated October 2026_

This policy applies to everyone who uses RetroTide, account holders and guests alike. It forms part of the [terms of service](/legal/terms).

## What is not allowed

Do not use RetroTide to:

- post anything illegal, or anything that encourages or helps someone break the law;
- harass, bully, threaten or abuse anyone;
- post hateful content about anyone's race, religion, sex, sexual orientation, disability or any other protected characteristic;
- post sexual content involving children, or any content that exploits or endangers children;
- share someone else's personal data without the right to do so;
- impersonate another person or organisation;
- send spam, or post links to malware or phishing pages;
- try to get into accounts, boards or data you have not been given access to, or to read hidden cards;
- probe, scan or attack RetroTide's systems, or get around its rate limits, its abuse caps or the fair-use limit on new boards;
- overload the service, for example with automated sign-ups or board joins.

## Reporting a problem

We do not filter what people type, so we rely on reports. If you see something on RetroTide that breaks this policy, email hello@retrotide.app.

Tell us the board link and what the problem is. We will look at every report.

## What facilitators can do

Facilitators run their own boards. They can remove a participant and delete cards. Removing a participant never deletes their cards, so a removal cannot reveal which cards were theirs.

## What happens if the policy is broken

Depending on how serious it is, we may:

- remove the content;
- remove a participant from a board;
- suspend or close the account or organisation responsible;
- report the matter to the police or another authority where the law requires it.
