# Cookie statement

_Last updated October 2026_

RetroTide uses strictly necessary cookies only. They are needed for the service to work, so we do not ask for consent and there is no cookie banner.

## The cookies we set

| Cookie | What it does | How long |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `__Host-rt_session` | Keeps you signed in after you sign in. | Until you close your browser, or 30 days if you turn on "Remember me"; cleared when you sign out |
| `__Host-rt_2fa` | Holds a sign-in that is waiting for your two-factor code. | 5 minutes, cleared when the code is accepted |
| `__Host-rt_trusted_device` | Lets this device skip the two-factor code, if you chose "Don't ask again on this device". | 30 days, or until you change your password or turn two-factor off |
| `__Host-rt_guest` | Remembers your guest device, so you can come back to a board you joined by link without an account. | 90 days |
| `__Host-rt_oidc` | Ties a Google sign-in to the browser that started it, so nobody else can finish it. | 10 minutes, cleared when the sign-in finishes |

Every one of them is set by RetroTide itself, is sent only to RetroTide, cannot be read by scripts on the page, and is sent only over an encrypted connection.

## What we do not use

- No analytics cookies.
- No advertising or tracking cookies.
- No third-party cookies.

Our usage numbers come from counters on our own servers and from Cloudflare's traffic statistics, neither of which uses cookies.

## Storage in your browser

The app also keeps a few small values in your browser's storage. They are not cookies, and they are never used for tracking:

- your theme choice and the organisation you last opened, kept until you clear them;
- while a tab is open, the board link you joined through and an invitation you are part-way through accepting, both cleared when the tab closes.
